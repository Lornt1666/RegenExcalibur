## Entering unfamiliar domains

The ability to learn is offered as a method—not as a false credential.

For a subject outside prior direct experience, the workflow is:

1. Define the decision, deliverable, audience, and evidence standard.
2. Build a terminology map, source hierarchy, stakeholder map, and risk register.
3. Separate authoritative sources from commentary, inference, and unsupported claims.
4. Produce a bounded first draft, prototype, comparison, or research package.
5. Test it against counterexamples, missing assumptions, failure modes, and edge cases.
6. Route regulated, licensed, safety-critical, or highly specialized conclusions to the appropriate qualified reviewer.
7. Revise, document, and deliver an inspectable result.

This approach allows rapid cross-domain contribution without pretending to be a licensed lawyer, engineer, architect, clinician, accountant, financial adviser, or security auditor.
