# PromptOS commercialization architecture

**Status:** proposed commercial model; no licence change is made by this document.

PromptOS is designed to support a public-discovery → demonstrated-value → paid-conversion path while preserving owner control over the PromptOS source, constitutional prompt architecture, and commercial rights.

## Revenue model

### 1. Free discovery layer

Public visitors may inspect the repository, architecture, examples, benchmark evidence, and documentation subject to the repository's effective licence terms. The free layer exists to demonstrate capability and generate qualified demand; it does not silently grant commercial exploitation rights.

### 2. Prompt engineering services

Paid work can include:

- prompt repair and reconstruction;
- PromptOS compilation and adaptation;
- model-specific prompt architecture;
- benchmark and evaluation design;
- agent/workflow specification;
- structured-output and schema design;
- prompt-security and injection-resilience review;
- domain-specific PromptOS module design.

### 3. AutoDesign / AutoEngineer services

Paid project packages can use PromptOS to convert a client's objective into a multidisciplinary design-and-engineering specification. Possible deliverables include requirements, architecture, option studies, risk registers, implementation plans, test plans, traceability matrices, and professional-review packages.

AI-generated engineering material must not be represented as licensed engineering, sealed drawings, permit approval, or professional certification unless the required qualified professional actually performs that review and provides the applicable approval.

### 4. Commercial PromptOS licence

A future owner-approved commercial licence may grant organizations defined rights to embed, modify, host, redistribute, or commercially exploit designated PromptOS components. Terms, scope, pricing, attribution, support, seats, deployment rights, and redistribution rights must be agreed explicitly.

Until such a licence is adopted, `LICENSE_STATUS.md` controls.

### 5. Enterprise implementation

Potential paid enterprise work:

- private deployment;
- organization-specific prompt constitutions;
- custom routers and domain modules;
- policy and approval gates;
- private benchmark suites;
- model-provider adapters;
- audit/evidence pipelines;
- integration with repositories, internal tools, and APIs;
- maintenance and version migration.

### 6. Evaluation and certification service

Offer paid independent evaluation packages for customer prompts and PromptOS deployments:

- deterministic conformance testing;
- hallucination and authority-boundary tests;
- prompt-injection tests;
- pairwise benchmark comparisons;
- regression reports;
- release manifests;
- remediation recommendations.

Do not describe these as regulated certifications unless a legally authorized certification framework actually applies.

### 7. Template and module packs

Owner-approved paid packs may include specialized PromptOS modules for areas such as:

- software and API architecture;
- construction and built-environment coordination;
- product design;
- research;
- business operations;
- technical documentation;
- creative production;
- education;
- compliance-oriented workflows.

The public repository may contain demonstrations while commercial packs provide deeper schemas, test suites, adapters, implementation guides, support, or organization-specific tailoring.
