# Operational Perfection Criterion

PromptOS does not use “perfect” as an unsupported universal claim.

For a defined model, task distribution, environment, and release standard, a candidate is operationally complete when:

- every hard requirement passes;
- no known tested alternative produces a material quality gain without an unacceptable increase in risk, cost, latency, or complexity;
- residual limitations are disclosed;
- the exact evidence and scope are frozen;
- the terminal state is truthful.

This is a Pareto and evidence criterion, not a declaration that improvement is metaphysically impossible.
