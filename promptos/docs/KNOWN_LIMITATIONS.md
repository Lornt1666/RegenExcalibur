# Known Limitations

1. AUTO routing is keyword-based and does not yet provide full semantic or multilingual coverage.
2. The normalized v3.2 constitution is not represented as a claim of a verbatim signed source export.
3. Deterministic tests validate compiler behaviour, not target-model obedience.
4. The committed static holdout is not an independent scientific holdout.
5. No live model provider is invoked by the RC.
6. No blinded pairwise benchmark has been completed.
7. No independent human review has been completed.
8. Source escaping reduces delimiter injection but does not prove universal model-level prompt-injection resistance.
9. The allowed consequential-action vocabulary is intentionally narrow and may require governed extension.
10. Exact token counts depend on the target tokenizer and are not computed by the provider-neutral core.
11. Context budget is currently enforced as a character ceiling, not a model-specific token ceiling.
12. Professional-domain correctness remains dependent on the target model, evidence, jurisdiction, and qualified review.
