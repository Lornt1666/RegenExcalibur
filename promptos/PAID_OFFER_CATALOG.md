# PromptOS paid offer catalog

**Status:** commercial positioning draft. Prices are not binding offers and should be adjusted per scope, jurisdiction, risk, and client requirements.

## Entry offers

### Prompt Repair

For broken, vague, contradictory, oversized, or weak prompts.

Deliverables:

- diagnosis;
- repaired master prompt;
- compact version;
- assumptions/blockers;
- validation checklist.

Suggested commercial structure: fixed-price micro-project.

### Prompt Architecture

For a reusable operational prompt, agent workflow, or model-specific instruction system.

Deliverables:

- requirements;
- modular architecture;
- tool/authority boundaries;
- schemas;
- evaluation cases;
- deployment prompt.

Suggested commercial structure: fixed-price project or milestone engagement.

## AutoDesign offers

### AutoDesign Concept Package

Turns a rough product, application, service, building concept, workflow, or invention into a structured design package.

Deliverables may include:

- user/problem definition;
- requirements;
- concept alternatives;
- selected architecture;
- UX/system flows;
- risks;
- implementation roadmap;
- acceptance criteria.

### AutoDesign Full Specification

Adds detailed system decomposition, interfaces, nonfunctional requirements, lifecycle assumptions, tests, and traceability.

## AutoEngineer offers

### AutoEngineer Coordination Package

Creates an engineering-style specification and cross-disciplinary coordination package for qualified review.

Deliverables may include:

- design basis;
- discipline matrix;
- standards/research checklist;
- requirements;
- interfaces;
- engineering assumptions;
- risk/failure-mode register;
- calculation plan;
- verification/inspection plan;
- implementation sequence;
- professional-review handoff.

This service does not substitute for legally required professional engineering, architectural, trade, inspection, permit, or authority approvals.

### AutoAudit

Independent-style review of a customer's prompt, design package, workflow, or AI-generated specification.

Deliverables:

- defect register;
- severity classification;
- evidence gaps;
- authority and tool issues;
- recommended remediations;
- revised package when included in scope.
